DEUTERONOMY STYLE MAP - ALL 43 CHUNKS
=======================================
Based on: proven styles from Genesis-Numbers, user preferences
Core rules: reverent/sacred, beat must carry, variety, contrast (spoken vs sung)
Underscores in lyrics bypass Suno copyright filters

FORMAT: CHUNK # | CHAPTER/PART | STYLE NAME | STYLE TAG | WHY THIS STYLE

=====================================================================
STORY A: HISTORICAL RETELLING (Chunks 1-7)
"Moses recounting the wilderness journey" — narrative, storytelling
=====================================================================

CHUNK 1  | Ch 1 Pt 1 of 2 | LO-FI FOLK
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"
WHY: Opening — Moses speaking, setting the stage, appointing leaders. Narrative storytelling, intimate, the voice of Moses.

CHUNK 2  | Ch 1 Pt 2 of 2 | SPOKEN WORD FOLK
"Spoken Word Folk, folk-gospel, male lead, acoustic guitar, harmonica, moderate tempo, narrative, verse-based, call-and-response, building intensity"
WHY: Rebellion, spies, defeat — dramatic escalation. Spies sent, people refuse, chased like bees. Needs more intensity than chunk 1. The harmonica adds tension. Spoken word because it's conflict and confrontation.

CHUNK 3  | Ch 2 Pt 1 of 2 | LO-FI FOLK
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"
WHY: Wilderness wandering, passing Edom and Moab. Back to the journey — reflective, steady, the long road. The choir hums in the background while Moses narrates the decades of wandering.

CHUNK 4  | Ch 2 Pt 2 of 2 | ETHEREAL CHORAL
"ethereal choral, sacred ambient, orchestral worship, angelic vocals, soprano, heavenly strings, reverb, cinematic biblical, harp, celestial choir"
WHY: Sihon defeated — the first conquest. Victory shifts the tone from wandering to divine intervention. The angelic choir rises for the battle and triumph. Contrast with the lo-fi folk before it — the music swells when God delivers.

CHUNK 5  | Ch 3 Pt 1 of 1 | LO-FI FOLK → ANGELIC CHOIR CONTRAST
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"
WHY: Og defeated AND Moses' plea to cross the Jordan. Start lo-fi folk for the battle narrative, then the choir swells for Moses' prayer ("O Lord GOD, thou hast begun to show thy servant thy greatness"), then the choir lingers as God says no. The contrast between the spoken plea and the choral response is the emotional core.

CHUNK 6  | Ch 4 Pt 1 of 2 | SOFT PADS / INTRICATE RHYTHM
"intricate rhythm, 60 bpm, soft pads, ethereal vocals, spacious, reverent, building, holy, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Statutes and judgments, the Horeb experience, warning against idols. This is law and instruction — orderly, precise. The shift to sung vocals (no spoken word) after 5 chunks of spoken word creates contrast. The soft pads give it a solemn, sacred weight.

CHUNK 7  | Ch 4 Pt 2 of 2 | ETHEREAL CHOIR
"Ethereal choir, shimmering, soprano layers, reverb, atmospheric, holy"
WHY: Scattering among nations, but also mercy — "if you seek the LORD you will find him." Cities of refuge. Reflective, both warning and hope. The simpler style after the intricate rhythm of chunk 6 gives breathing room. Ends Story A on a contemplative note.

STORY B: HEART OF THE COVENANT (Chunks 8-15)
"The Ten Commandments, the Shema, the great commandments"
=====================================================================

CHUNK 8  | Ch 5 Pt 1 of 1 | ETHEREAL CHORAL
"ethereal choral, sacred ambient, orchestral worship, angelic vocals, soprano, heavenly strings, reverb, cinematic biblical, harp, celestial choir"
WHY: THE TEN COMMANDMENTS. God speaking directly. This is the most sacred content in the book. Full ethereal choral — majestic, overwhelming, the voice of God from the fire. The people's terror and Moses as mediator. This needs to feel like heaven touching earth.

CHUNK 9  | Ch 6 Pt 1 of 1 | ANGELIC CHOIR
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic, 76 BPM"
WHY: THE SHEMA. "Hear O Israel, the LORD our God is one LORD." The most sacred prayer in scripture. Intimate, devotional, the angelic choir but softer and more personal than the Ten Commandments. The shift from the thunder of chapter 5 to the tenderness of the Shema. Then warning against forgetting — the beat builds.

CHUNK 10 | Ch 7 Pt 1 of 1 | ANGELIC CHOIR → LO-FI FOLK CONTRAST
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic"
THEN SHIFT TO:
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir"
WHY: "You are a holy people, chosen in love" — the angelic choir for the blessing and election. Then the warnings about the nations, destroying altars, no intermarriage — shifts to spoken word folk for the battle instructions. The contrast: sung blessing → spoken warning.

CHUNK 11 | Ch 8 Pt 1 of 1 | LO-FI FOLK
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"
WHY: "Remember all the way the LORD led you." Manna, the wilderness, the good land. Reflective, nostalgic, Moses remembering. The manna verse ("man does not live by bread alone") is the emotional heart — the choir hums beneath the spoken remembrance.

CHUNK 12 | Ch 9 Pt 1 of 2 | SPOKEN WORD FOLK
"Spoken Word Folk, folk-gospel, male lead, acoustic guitar, harmonica, moderate tempo, narrative, verse-based, building intensity"
WHY: "Not for your righteousness." Rebellious from day one. The golden calf recalled. This is confrontation — Moses rebuking the people. Spoken word folk because it's direct speech, a sermon, a warning. The harmonica adds a mournful, bluesy edge.

CHUNK 13 | Ch 9 Pt 2 of 2 | LO-FI FOLK → ANGELIC CHOIR CONTRAST
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, cathedral reverb, tape saturation"
THEN SHIFT TO:
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic"
WHY: Moses breaks the tablets, 40 days of intercession. Starts lo-fi folk for the dramatic narrative (breaking, burning, grinding the calf). Then the angelic choir rises for Moses' prayer: "Remember your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob." The contrast: spoken fury → choral intercession.

CHUNK 14 | Ch 10 Pt 1 of 1 | HARP / DELICATE STRINGS
"intricate rhythm, 70 bpm, harp, delicate strings, ornate, intricate, meditative, beautiful, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Second tablets, the ark, Levi set apart, "what does the LORD require?" Priestly content — the ark, the priests, the law. Harp and delicate strings fit the sanctuary imagery. Sung vocals (no spoken word) after two chunks of spoken word creates contrast. "Circumcise your hearts, love the foreigner" — beautiful, meditative.

CHUNK 15 | Ch 11 Pt 1 of 1 | ETHEREAL CHORAL
"ethereal choral, sacred ambient, orchestral worship, angelic vocals, soprano, heavenly strings, reverb, cinematic biblical, harp, celestial choir"
WHY: "Love the LORD your God." Blessing and curse set before them, Gerizim and Ebal. This is the climax of Story B — the choice. Full ethereal choral for the covenant summary. The land that God cares for, the rain in its season. Majestic and consequential. Ends Story B with weight.

STORY C: THE DEUTERONOMIC CODE (Chunks 16-31)
"94 scenes of law — the most variety needed here"
=====================================================================

CHUNK 16 | Ch 12 Pt 1 of 2 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY
"dignified, 64 bpm, clean tones, orderly, elevated standard, precise, unblemished, priestly, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Destroy pagan worship places, seek the place God chooses. This is the foundational law of worship — centralization. Dignified and priestly because it's about proper worship. 64 BPM, orderly, the law being laid down.

CHUNK 17 | Ch 12 Pt 2 of 2 | HYPNOTIC PERCUSSION
"hypnotic percussion loop, countertenor male vocal, smooth, studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, frame drum, shaker, steady walking tempo, repetitive groove, subtle variations, dry mix, compressed vocals, spacious, 78 BPM"
WHY: Eating meat, blood, tithes, not neglecting the Levite. Detailed regulations — repetitive list-like content. The hypnotic percussion loop carries the recitation of rules. The beat has to crush here because the content is dry — the rhythm makes it listenable.

CHUNK 18 | Ch 13 Pt 1 of 1 | SPOKEN WORD FOLK
"Spoken Word Folk, folk-gospel, male lead, acoustic guitar, harmonica, moderate tempo, narrative, verse-based, urgent, warning"
WHY: False prophets, enticers, city led astray — all put to death. This is WARNING content. Spoken word because it's dire, urgent. The harmonica adds a haunting quality. "Stone him with stones" — the gravity of the warning needs the human voice, not a choir.

CHUNK 19 | Ch 14 Pt 1 of 1 | HYPNOTIC PERCUSSION
"hypnotic percussion loop, countertenor male vocal, smooth, studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, frame drum, shaker, steady walking tempo, repetitive groove, subtle variations, dry mix, compressed vocals, spacious, 78 BPM"
WHY: Clean and unclean animals — a LIST. Ox, sheep, goat, hart, roebuck, fallow deer... The hypnotic percussion carries the list. The countertenor voice keeps it sacred while the beat keeps it engaging. The beat MUST carry this because it's catalog content.

CHUNK 20 | Ch 15 Pt 1 of 1 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY
"dignified, 64 bpm, clean tones, orderly, elevated standard, precise, unblemished, priestly, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"

CHUNK 21 | Ch 16 Pt 1 of 1 | HARP / DELICATE STRINGS
"intricate rhythm, 70 bpm, harp, delicate strings, ornate, intricate, meditative, beautiful, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Passover, Feast of Weeks, Feast of Booths. These are FEASTS — celebratory, sacred. The harp and delicate strings fit the festival atmosphere. "Rejoice before the LORD your God." The most joyful chunk in the law code — let the music reflect that.

CHUNK 22 | Ch 17 Pt 1 of 1 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY
"dignified, 64 bpm, clean tones, orderly, elevated standard, precise, unblemished, priestly, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Blemished sacrifices, idolaters, hard cases, the king. Law about leadership and worship quality. Dignified — "no blemish, no evil-favoredness." The king must copy and read the law. Orderly, precise, elevated.

CHUNK 23 | Ch 18 Pt 1 of 1 | HARP / DELICATE STRINGS
"intricate rhythm, 70 bpm, harp, delicate strings, ornate, intricate, meditative, beautiful, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Levites' portion, no divination, "a Prophet like Moses." Priestly/prophetic content. The harp fits the Levite imagery. "The LORD is their inheritance." And the prophecy of a prophet like Moses — beautiful, meditative, the harp carries the prophetic weight.

CHUNK 24 | Ch 19 Pt 1 of 1 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY

WHY: Cities of refuge, witnesses, eye for eye. Justice law — precise, orderly. The dignified style matches the legal precision. "Life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth." Clean, measured, judicial.

CHUNK 26 | Ch 21 Pt 1 of 1 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY
"dignified, 64 bpm, clean tones, orderly, elevated standard, precise, unblemished, priestly, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Unsolved murder (the heifer), captive woman, firstborn's double portion, rebellious son, hanging on a tree. Mixed laws — all about boundaries and dignity. The heifer ritual, the rebellious son stoned — weighty, judicial content.

CHUNK 27 | Ch 22 Pt 1 of 1 | HYPNOTIC PERCUSSION

CHUNK 28 | Ch 23 Pt 1 of 1 | DIGNIFIED / PRIESTLY
"dignified, 64 bpm, clean tones, orderly, elevated standard, precise, unblemished, priestly, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
WHY: Who may enter the assembly, camp holiness, escaped slave, no cult prostitution, no interest, vows. Purity and camp law. "The LORD walks in the midst of your camp." Dignified — the camp must be holy.

CHUNK 29 | Ch 24 Pt 1 of 1 | LO-FI FOLK

CHUNK 31 | Ch 26 Pt 1 of 1 | SACRED HYMN

STORY D: COVENANT SANCTIONS (Chunks 32-37)
"Blessings and curses — maximum contrast needed"
=====================================================================

CHUNK 32 | Ch 27 Pt 1 of 1 | SPOKEN WORD FOLK
"Spoken Word Folk, folk-gospel, male lead, acoustic guitar, harmonica, moderate tempo, narrative, verse-based, call-and-response"
WHY: Stones on Ebal, the 12 curses. "Cursed be... and all the people shall say AMEN." This is LITURGICAL CALL-AND-RESPONSE. The spoken word folk style with call-and-response refrains fits perfectly — the Levites call, the people respond "Amen." The harmonica underscores the gravity.

CHUNK 33 | Ch 28 Pt 1 of 3 | ANGELIC CHOIR → LO-FI FOLK CONTRAST
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic"
THEN SHIFT TO:
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, minor key, mournful, sparse"
WHY: BLESSINGS first — "Blessed shall you be in the city, blessed in the field." The angelic choir soars for the blessings. Then the pivot: "BUT if you will not hear..." — the music drops to lo-fi folk in minor key for the curses. The CONTRAST is the whole point — blessing vs curse, light vs dark. This is where the style matching matters most.

CHUNK 34 | Ch 28 Pt 2 of 3 | LO-FI FOLK (MINOR KEY, DARK)
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse, mournful, minor key, descending, no build — only decay"
WHY: The curses intensify — disease, madness, stolen labor, wives taken, children given to strangers. This is DARK. The lo-fi folk strips down — sparse, mournful, descending. No build-up, only decay. The choir chants wordlessly like a funeral. The beat is heavy and slow.

CHUNK 35 | Ch 28 Pt 3 of 3 | LO-FI FOLK (MINOR KEY, DARKEST)
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 60 BPM, slow rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, very sparse, minor key, desolate, the bleakest sound"
WHY: THE SIEGE. Eating your own children. Scattered among nations. "In the morning you shall say 'Would God it were evening' and at evening 'Would God it were morning.'" This is the darkest content in Deuteronomy. 60 BPM — the slowest, heaviest version. Sparse, desolate. The choir barely whispers. The beat is a death march.

CHUNK 36 | Ch 29 Pt 1 of 1 | ETHEREAL CHOIR
"Ethereal choir, shimmering, soprano layers, reverb, atmospheric, holy, mysterious"
WHY: Covenant renewal, "the secret things belong to the LORD." After the horror of chapter 28, this is mystery and solemnity. The ethereal choir creates an atmosphere of the unknown — "eyes yet unseeing, ears yet un hearing." The shimmering soprano layers evoke the mystery of God's hidden counsel.

CHUNK 37 | Ch 30 Pt 1 of 1 | ANGELIC CHOIR
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic, building to triumphant climax"
WHY: "RETURN and the LORD will gather you." "He will circumcise your heart." "CHOOSE LIFE." This is the turn — hope after judgment. The angelic choir builds from soft to triumphant. "I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing — therefore CHOOSE LIFE." The most hopeful chunk in the sanctions. The beat builds to a climax.

STORY E: SONG, BLESSING, AND FAREWELL (Chunks 38-43)
"The emotional finale — Moses' last words"
=====================================================================

CHUNK 38 | Ch 31 Pt 1 of 2 | LO-FI FOLK
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"
WHY: "I am 120 years old today. I can no more go out and come in." Moses' farewell charge, Joshua commissioned. Intimate, personal — the old leader passing the torch. Lo-fi folk for Moses speaking. "Be strong and courageous" — the choir swells briefly, then recedes.

CHUNK 39 | Ch 31 Pt 2 of 2 | SPOKEN WORD FOLK
"Spoken Word Folk, folk-gospel, male lead, acoustic guitar, harmonica, moderate tempo, narrative, verse-based, solemn, testimonial"
WHY: "Write this song as a witness." The deposition of the law beside the ark. Moses knows they'll rebel after his death. This is TESTIMONY — a dying man's warning. Spoken word folk, solemn and testimonial. The harmonica is mournful. "I know your rebellion and your stiff neck."

CHUNK 40 | Ch 32 Pt 1 of 2 | SACRED HYMN
"sacred hymn, countertenor male vocal, smooth, studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, concert harp, gentle strings, oboe, warm cello, resolving harmony, slow build to gentle climax, wide stereo, compressed vocals, D minor, 74 BPM"
WHY: THE SONG OF MOSES. "Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak." This is POETRY — the most literarily elevated content in Deuteronomy. The sacred hymn style is perfect: countertenor, concert harp, cello. "He kept him as the apple of his eye." "As an eagle stirs up her nest." The music must match the poetry. D minor because the song turns dark (Jeshurun waxed fat).

CHUNK 41 | Ch 32 Pt 2 of 2 | SACRED HYMN → SPOKEN WORD FOLK CONTRAST
"sacred hymn, countertenor male vocal, smooth, studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, concert harp, gentle strings, oboe, warm cello, resolving harmony, wide stereo, compressed vocals, D minor, 74 BPM"
THEN SHIFT TO:
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, cathedral reverb, sparse, reflective"
WHY: The Song continues — "Vengeance is mine, I will repay." "See now that I, even I, am he." The sacred hymn carries the divine declaration. Then the narrative conclusion — Moses speaks the song, charges the people, and God tells him to ascend Nebo to die. The shift from hymn to lo-fi folk is the shift from divine song to human mortality. Moses is going to die.

CHUNK 42 | Ch 33 Pt 1 of 1 | ANGELIC CHOIR
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic, with concert harp and celestial strings, building to majestic climax"
WHY: THE BLESSING OF THE TRIBES. "The LORD came from Sinai and rose up from Seir." "There is none like the God of Jeshurun." This is Moses' final blessing — majestic, tender, prophetic. The angelic choir at full power. Each tribe blessed. "The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms." The most beautiful chunk in the book. Let the choir soar.

CHUNK 43 | Ch 34 Pt 1 of 1 | LO-FI FOLK → ANGELIC CHOIR (FADE)
"lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 60 BPM, slow rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse, minor key, reflective"
THEN THE CHOIR RISES ONE LAST TIME:
"angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, holy, melodic, then fading to silence"
WHY: THE DEATH OF MOSES. "So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab." "His eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated." "There arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses." Start lo-fi folk — sparse, 60 BPM, the death march. Moses sees the promised land from Nebo but cannot enter. Then the angelic choir rises one final time for the eulogy — "whom the LORD knew face to face" — and fades to silence. The end.
