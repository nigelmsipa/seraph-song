==========================================
CROSS-BOOK STYLE ANALYSIS
==========================================

1. EVOLUTION OVER TIME:
   - EARLIEST (7-8 months ago): Uploads with detailed acoustic folk-gospel descriptions (Genesis 29-30). Pre-v5 Suno or external uploads.
   - EARLY v5 (5-7 months ago): Simple tag-based styles ("angelic choir", "Ethereal choir, shimmering, soprano layers, reverb, atmospheric, holy"). Exodus and Leviticus started here.
   - MID v5 (3-5 months ago): More detailed tags with BPM and instrumentation ("intricate rhythm, 60/70 bpm, soft pads/harp, delicate strings..."). Numbers started here.
   - LATEST v5.5 (1-2 months ago): Two approaches — (a) long cinematic descriptive prompts, (b) highly detailed production tags ("lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity")

2. RECURRING STYLE FAMILIES (used across multiple books):

   a) "ETHEREAL CHORAL" family (Genesis, Leviticus, Numbers):
      "ethereal choral, sacred ambient, orchestral worship, angelic vocals, soprano, heavenly strings, reverb, cinematic biblical, harp, celestial choir"
   
   b) "SOFT PADS / INTRICATE RHYTHM" family (Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers):
      "intricate rhythm, [60-76] bpm, soft pads, ethereal vocals, spacious, reverent, building, holy, sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word"
   
   c) "ANGELIC CHOIR" family (Genesis, Numbers):
      "angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal, spacious, reverent, building, holy, melodic"
   
   d) "SACRED HYMN / COUNTERTENOR" family (Numbers only):
      "sacred hymn, countertenor male vocal, smooth, studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, concert harp..."
   
   e) "LO-FI FOLK / DEVOTIONAL SPOKEN WORD" family (Genesis v5.5 + ATTR workspace):
      "lo-fi folk, devotional spoken word, 74 BPM, rhythmic speech, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed cajón, bowed double bass, hand drum pulse, unison chapel choir, wordless male chants, cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build, call-and-response refrains, minor key, reflective solemnity"

3. KEY VOCAL DIRECTIVES:
   - "sung vocals, melodic, no spoken word" (Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers)
   - "Spoken Word Folk" (Genesis 31)
   - "devotional spoken word, rhythmic speech" (Genesis v5.5)
   - "countertenor male vocal" (Numbers)
   - "boy choir" (Numbers)
   - "angelic vocals, soprano" (Genesis, Leviticus)
   - "unison chapel choir, wordless male chants" (Genesis v5.5)

4. BPM PATTERNS:
   - 60 BPM: Exodus (soft pads style)
   - 64 BPM: Leviticus (dignified style), Genesis v5.5 (lo-fi folk)
   - 70 BPM: Exodus (harp style)
   - 74 BPM: Numbers (sacred hymn), Genesis v5.5 (lo-fi folk)
   - 76 BPM: Numbers (angelic choir)
   - 78 BPM: Numbers (hypnotic percussion)
   - 120 BPM: Numbers (experimental upload)

5. MUSICAL KEYS:
   - Minor key mentioned for: Genesis v5.5 ("minor key, reflective solemnity"), Numbers ("D minor")
   - Major key mentioned for: Genesis uploads ("major key"), Numbers ("F major", "E Major")
   - Most songs don't specify key

6. PRODUCTION QUALITIES:
   - EARLY: "clean production, natural sound, intimate, warm" (Genesis uploads)
   - MID: "reverb, atmospheric, spacious" (Exodus, Leviticus)
   - LATE: "studio quality, high fidelity, polished production, wide stereo, compressed vocals" (Numbers)
   - LATEST: "cathedral reverb, tape saturation, vinyl crackle, sparse-to-dense build" (Genesis v5.5)

7. WHAT WORKED (based on consistency and repetition):
   - The "intricate rhythm" style was reused extensively across Exodus → Numbers, suggesting it produced good results
   - The "ethereal choral" style was reused from Genesis → Leviticus, suggesting satisfaction
   - The "lo-fi folk / devotional spoken word" style appears to be the LATEST evolution, used in the most recent Genesis v5.5 work and the ATTR workspace — this seems to be the current preferred direction

8. WHAT DIDN'T WORK / EXPERIMENTS:
   - "lll" songs in Numbers 2 with no styles (v5.5) — appear to be tests/experiments
   - "boy choir" — very simple tag, used multiple times in Numbers but not carried forward
   - "Bulgarian choir" — used once in Numbers, not repeated
   - Genesis uploads (pre-v5 acoustic folk-gospel) — approach abandoned in favor of Suno generation
   - "Spoken Word Folk" — used once in Genesis, not repeated in later books
