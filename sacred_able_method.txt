THE SACRED-ABLE METHOD - BEAT WORKFLOW
========================================

SACRED-ABLE: A double meaning.
  1. Sacred-able — any beat can be made sacred, transformed into something usable for scripture.
  2. Sacred Hevel — Hevel (הֶבֶל) is Abel's Hebrew name, meaning vapor, breath, fleeting.
     The word used throughout Ecclesiastes: "vanity of vanities, all is hevel."
     The Bible is hevel — breath spoken thousands of years ago, long gone —
     and this method catches that breath and makes it sacred.
     The offering that rises. The beat that's fleeting but catches something eternal.

PSYCHOANALYSIS: The user is a "folk hopper" — lives in the tension between folk warmth (handpans, acoustic guitar) and hip-hop beats (lo-fi, trap, trip hop). The golden tool isn't one perfect beat — it's any beat that sits in the folk-hop intersection: warm enough to be sacred, heavy enough to be a banger.

SACRED-ABLE: Take ANY source material (classical, trap, lo-fi, handpan, cinematic, other people's Suno songs) and transform it into something sacred. The source doesn't matter as long as the beat hits. Make it sacred-able. Make the fleeting sacred.

WORKFLOW:
1. Collect beats from anywhere — "collecting stones like Dinos"
   - Search Suno for other people's songs with good beats
   - Any genre is fair game: classical, trap, lo-fi, ambient, folk
   - The only filter: does the beat grab your ear?
2. Cover/transform the beat with a sacred style tag
   - ":( horror x trip hop ): alone & scared" (dark sacred)
   - "angelic choir, soft pads, ethereal..." (bright sacred)
   - "sacred hymn, countertenor..." (priestly sacred)
   - The transformation makes it usable for scripture
3. Banger test — does it still hit after being made sacred?
4. Feed the sacred beat into a Bible chunk with lyrics
5. If it hits — it's a banger. If not — iterate or find a new beat

BEAT SOURCING AVENUES:
A. Suno search — find other people's songs, cover them (NEW MAIN AVENUE)
   - Suno allows searching other users' creations
   - Find a beat that grabs you, transform it to angelic choir
   - "Now it's usable"
B. Existing uploads — beats already in Polaris and other workspaces
   - Can't upload new YouTube beats anymore (Suno cracked down)
   - What's there is there
C. Generate from scratch — hardest path, rarely hits
   - Suno's beat generation is inconsistent
   - The beat is what carries the song — if it doesn't hit, nothing works
