KEY INSIGHT:
The "horror x trip hop" style tag is the secret sauce for transforming uploaded beats.
It adds darkness, atmosphere, and weight to the raw YouTube beats.
The cover function in Suno takes the uploaded audio and reimagines it with this style.

THE GOLDEN TOOL PROBLEM:
- Generating a good beat from scratch in Suno is extremely difficult
- The beat is the foundation — if the beat doesn't hit, nothing else works
- YouTube-sourced beats provide a higher quality foundation than Suno-generated beats
- The goal: create a systematic way to produce beats that consistently hit
- This is "impossible" — but that's what we're trying to solve

BEAT TYPES USED:
1. Lo-fi hip hop (85 BPM, Rhodes, vinyl crackle) — for reflective/narrative content
2. Trap (140 BPM, 808 bass, hi-hats) — for urgent/intense content
3. Handpan/folk acoustic (88-104 BPM) — for sacred/meditative content
4. Horror trip hop (Suno cover style) — for dark/dramatic content
