VISION:
The project treats all sacred and serious text as hevel — breath, fleeting —
and catches it in music before it's gone. Bible, theology, philosophy, devotionals —
all of it can be made sacred-able. The beat carries it, the choir elevates it,
and the banger test decides if it stays.

The Memory Method Bible framework (296 stories, 7,962 scenes across all 66 books)
provides the structural foundation for the Bible portion. Other texts use their own
chapter/section divisions, chunked to fit Suno's ~4000 character limit.
